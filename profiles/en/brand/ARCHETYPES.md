# The 12 archetypes (from the English archetype test, ikigaiguide.dk/en/archetypes)

Use the names exactly as written here. The core line may be quoted word for word. Everything else about an
archetype must be a recognisable observation, never a claim about what the test "proves". The test has one
version for men and one for women. The same motive has two names, e.g. The King and The Queen.

| Motive | Men | Core (men) | Women | Core (women) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ruler | The King | You create order and take responsibility. | The Queen | You create order and stand firm in your own worth. |
| warrior | The Warrior | You get stronger from resistance. | The Huntress | You set a goal and go after it. |
| sage | The Sage | You want to understand before you act. | The Wise Woman | You look for the truth beneath the surface. |
| magician | The Magician | You see patterns and create change. | The Mystic | You sense what others can't put into words. |
| lover | The Lover | You live through presence and passion. | The Muse | You bring life, beauty and warmth into other people's lives. |
| explorer | The Explorer | You need freedom and new horizons. | The Wild Woman | You need freedom and room to be yourself. |
| rebel | The Rebel | You challenge what everyone else takes for granted. | The Rebel | You refuse to follow rules that make no sense. |
| creator | The Creator | You need to make something that didn't exist before. | The Artist | You need to create something that carries your mark. |
| caregiver | The Protector | You look after the people who matter. | The Mother | You give security and look after others. |
| idealist | The Idealist | You believe in the good and want to do what's right. | The Dreamer | You believe in the good and hold on to hope. |
| jester | The Jester | You make life lighter for everyone around you. | The Spark | You bring energy and laughter wherever you go. |
| everyman | The Everyman | You're the one people can count on. | The Best Friend | You're the glue in the group. |

## Formats that work

- Recognition: "The Rebel in a meeting." 2 to 3 lines of behaviour, last line a question.
- Choice: "King. Explorer. Jester. Which one are you?" People answer in the comments.
- Contrast: "The Magician vs the Idealist."
- Everyday humour: "The Protector at a party."
- Status and self-worth: what each archetype actually wants, as opposed to what it pretends to want.
